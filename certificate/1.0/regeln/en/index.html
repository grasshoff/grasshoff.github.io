Rules of examination, validation and editingdraft for discussion

Famulus Certificate FC 1.0 · 2026-11-21 · Deutsch · Deed · Specification

The certificate states that the design of the content, the argument, the thesis and the structure of a publication come from its author, and that every draft text was edited by the author, who answers for it. Famulus works on a text only under the following rules; every rule has a fixed id, and every finding under a rule is recorded with it (element AU).

Authorship (element AU)

The design of the content, the argument, the thesis and the structure of the document come from the author; every draft text is edited by the author and answered for by him; Famulus has worked only under the editing rules of this certificate (rules B, S, T).

AU1The authorship record names the author's sources of the text (recording of a talk, dictation, manuscript, outline, annotations), each with its SHA-256.checker
AU2The theses and the sections of the document are listed, each with origin 'author' and the source it comes from; a thesis or section of machine origin fails.checker
AU3The editing record lists every version with its SHA-256 and counts the author's notes and the proposals taken over, discarded and edited by hand; no machine change entered the text without the author taking it over.checker
AU4Every finding under the rules of this certificate is recorded with its rule and decided; an undecided finding leaves the condition open.checker
AU5The author has confirmed the authorship record and released exactly this text (text_sha).author

B · Editing by Famulus

What Famulus may and may not do to the author's text.

idruleexamined byelement
B1Famulus adds no content, thesis or evaluation that does not come from the author (recording, dictation, manuscript, note).
implemented in: annotator (Anweisung), AU2		authorAU
B2Structure, outline and order of the document change only on a note of the author.
implemented in: annotator.planen (Reichweite), Redaktionsprotokoll		programAU
B3No content word is replaced by one of another meaning; names, dates and quotations stay as they are unless the author asks otherwise.
implemented in: annotator (Anweisung); Befund bei Abweichung		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)AU
B4The author's note takes precedence over every general rule.
implemented in: revision.yaml anweisung.vorrang		programAU
B5Where the content for a change is missing, Famulus returns the passage unchanged and says what is missing; nothing is invented.
implemented in: annotator (Nachfrage, kein leerer Vorschlag)		programAU
B6Every change by Famulus is a proposal; it enters the text only when the author takes it over (replace or by hand).
implemented in: outputs (Vorschlag), Ersetzen nur durch den Autor; AU3		programAU
B7In the reading text of a talk, remarks on operating, turning slides, time and the room are left out; nothing is added that was not said.
implemented in: vortrag.pruefen_code (saalbemerkung, zeitmarke); Lesetext-Regeln		programAU
B8Figures come from recorded facts or from sources; no figure is estimated or rounded without saying so.
implemented in: news_lektorat.merge ({{key}}); quellen		programAU

Q · Sources and evidence

Every statement resting on a source is checked at the source.

idruleexamined byelement
Q1Every verbatim quotation is checked at its source (transcript or facsimile); a rendering is marked as such.
implemented in: famulus-notebook quellen.check_quote (Transkript, Faksimile)		at the sourceGR
Q2Every picture credit is checked at the catalogue of the holding institution (inventory number, maker, year, rights).
implemented in: quellen.check_picture (Museums-Katalog)		at the sourceGR
Q3Every attribution (who writes to whom) agrees with caption and source.
implemented in: vortrag.pruefen_code (zuschreibung)		programGR
Q4Every fr: address resolves and is pinned.
implemented in: Famulus-Referenz-Resolver; GR1, GR2		programGR
Q5Dates and names are checked against the source; what cannot be checked stays a finding with its reason.
implemented in: quellen.pruefe; Befund bei Nichtprüfbarkeit		at the sourceGR

S · Language and form

Syntax, spelling and register by the standard references of the language and by LWgrammar.

idruleexamined byelement
S1Syntax: complete, grammatically correct sentences; no broken sentences or anacolutha from the spoken text.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_rule_conformity (syntax); pruefregeln		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)EX
S2Spelling and punctuation by the standard of the language: German by Duden (official rules), English by Oxford (Shorter Oxford English Dictionary, New Hart's Rules), French, Italian and Spanish by their academies; together with the rules of LWgrammar (famulus-writingguide-1).
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_writingguide_check; pruefregeln		programEX
S3Register and style follow the series' language form and the author's style; no stock phrases, no evaluation without evidence.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_sprachformen, Stilprofil des Autors		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)EX
S4Generic masculine in German; no gender signs (colon, asterisk, underscore, internal capital, slash) and no paired forms; the standard form in other languages as well.
implemented in: pruefregeln (Muster); Quarto-Vorlage arxivai (language)		programEX

L · Notes, literature, references

Notes and bibliographical references are complete, resolvable and checked.

idruleexamined byelement
L1Every footnote or side note belongs to a place and documents or explains it; no orphaned and no duplicate notes.
implemented in: pruefregeln (Fußnoten)		programGR
L2Every bibliographical reference resolves in the bibliography; every entry is complete (author, title, year, place or journal, pages) and checked at a catalogue (DOI, ISBN, library catalogue); the citation style follows the series.
implemented in: pruefregeln (Literatur: .bib ↔ Zitate, Katalog)		programGR
L3Every reference to a Famulus collection is a pinned fr: address with its cited content.
implemented in: GR1, GR2		programGR
L4No bibliography entry without a citation in the text and no citation without an entry.
implemented in: pruefregeln (Literatur)		programGR

A · Quality of argument

Thesis, structure and soundness of the argument.

idruleexamined byelement
A1The thesis is stated explicitly and comes from the author.
implemented in: AU2		authorAU
A2The structure of the argument is reconstructed as a graph; every claim is grounded, marked as a thesis or recorded as a gap.
implemented in: Famulus-Lektor (Argumentrekonstruktion); CL, AR		programAR
A3Quality of structure: every step has its kind (deductive, defeasible, computational, explanatory); no circle; no gap between premises and conclusion; no conclusion asserts more than its grounds carry.
implemented in: famulus-proof (resolution, derive, Validitätsbaum); AR2		programAR
A4For every explanatory claim rivals and discriminating cases are named.
implemented in: AR3		programAR
A5The claims of the text do not contradict one another.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_logical_relations (contradicts)		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)AR
A6The outline carries the argument: every section has its function in the structure (question, finding, justification, conclusion); the author confirms it.
implemented in: AU2		authorAU

T · Concepts and terminology

Terms are introduced, used consistently and checked against the terminological references.

idruleexamined byelement
T1Every technical term is introduced at its first use (definition or reference).
implemented in: pruefregeln (Einführung); LWgrammar lw_lector		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)EX
T2Every term is used consistently: the same term in the same sense, no silent shift of meaning, no changing words for the same concept.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_lector (Begriffsinventar, Bedeutungen)		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)EX
T3Terms are checked against the terminological references of LWgrammar (rules R-GG and R-LG, technical dictionaries, registers); meaning is relative to author, work and period.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_term, lw_usage_rules (R-GG, R-LG), Fachwörterbücher		programEX
T4Historical terms stand in the meaning of their time, with evidence.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_consult_understanding		language model (a reading, signed by a referee)EX
T5A blocking violation of a LWgrammar rule is a blocking finding.
implemented in: LWgrammar lw_gate (0 blockierende Konflikte)		programAU

V · Procedure and record

How the editing is recorded.

idruleexamined byelement
V1Every version is frozen and identified by its hash.
implemented in: vortrag.festhalten, news_lektorat (fassungen/)		programAU
V2Every note and every decision is in the journal.
implemented in: annotations (Journal anmerkungen.jsonl)		programAU
V3A blocking finding prevents the release.
implemented in: vortrag.freigeben, publikation.start		programAU
V4The output of a language model is a reading, not a check; it is on the residual list with model and hashes.
implemented in: IC3		programIC
V5Machine translations are declared as such and carry the state of their source.
implemented in: AC2; Veröffentlichen-Ansicht (Stand der Sprachfassungen)		programAC

P · Publication

What holds before and at publication.

idruleexamined byelement
P1Only the shown, released version (its hash) is published.
implemented in: lecture_publish.run, news_publish.run (sha)		programAC
P2Every page carries imprint, counter and – by the assignment rule – its METIS counting mark.
implemented in: famulus_publisher, metis (Vergaberegel)		programAC
P3No page contains local paths or internal addresses.
implemented in: Leck-Gate (export_lettre._leaks)		programAC
P4Where and at which address a page appears is shown to the author before the release.
implemented in: Veröffentlichen-Ansicht (URL, Fassung, Sprachen)		authorAC
